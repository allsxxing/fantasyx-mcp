# Trade Veto Process

From the 2025 Sleeper settings (carry forward unless changed for 2026):

- **Trade review:** 2 days. A trade processes automatically after the review window unless vetoed.
- **Veto threshold:** 5 votes needed to overturn a trade.
- **Trade deadline:** Week 11.

## Flow

1. Trade is agreed in Sleeper → review window opens.
2. Commissioner posts the trade-veto poll (see `chat-templates/trade-veto-poll.md`).
3. If 5+ veto votes land inside the window, the trade is killed; otherwise it processes.
4. Commissioner records the outcome.
