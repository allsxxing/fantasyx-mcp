# ❌ Champion — Weekly Admin Workflow

The ❌ is commissioner-enforced; Sleeper has no native support. Keep the belt honest
by logging every week to `content/x-champion-log.json`.

## Week 1

1. After scores finalize, the **highest-scoring team in the whole league** is crowned the first ❌ Champion.
2. Tiebreaker: head-to-head from Week 1 → bench points → commissioner coin flip.
3. Log the holder for Week 1.

## Week 2 and beyond

1. **Monday** — after scores finalize, announce the new/continuing ❌ Champion (crowning template).
2. **By Friday 11:59 PM CT** — the holder publicly names one FLEX-eligible player on their upcoming opponent. That player is locked into the opponent's starting FLEX (can't be benched). No declaration = no lock that week.
3. **Enforce** — if the opponent benches the locked player, note it in the Monday recap (or the league honors the lock via manual scoring).
4. **Transfer** — holder wins → keeps the belt; holder loses → the team that beat them inherits it.
5. **Log it** — record holder, locked player, declared_at, on-time?, and result each week.

> It's **sabotage, not theft** — the player goes into the *opponent's* lineup, not the holder's. (Per V4; provisional and subject to change.)
