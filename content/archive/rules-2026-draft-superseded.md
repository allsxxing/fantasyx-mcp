### 2. X CHAMPIONSHIP / X FACTOR (THE REAL LEAGUE KILLER)
This mechanic exists to murder any "Week 1 winner runs the table" narrative and keep every single team locked in from Week 1 to the championship.

**How It Works:**

- **Week 1:** The single **highest scoring team** in the entire league is crowned the first **X Champion**.

- **Week 2 and every week after:**
  - The current X Champion gets to **select ONE player** from the roster of the team they are matched up against that week.
  - That player (starter **or** bench) gets placed into **ONE of the X Champion's FLEX spots** for scoring purposes that week.
  - **Eligible:** Only players on the opponent's current roster.
  - **NOT eligible:** IR, Waivers, Free Agents, or anyone not on that specific team's roster.

- **Title Transfer Rule:** If the X Champion **loses** their matchup that week, the team that beat them **immediately inherits the X Championship** for the following week.

**Why This Slaps:**
- The crown can change hands every single week.
- No team gets to coast after a hot start.
- Forces everybody to stay dialed in on matchups, waivers, and opponent rosters.
- Trash talk writes itself. "I stole your RB1 and still cooked you."
- Keeps the whole league enthusiastic and cutthroat all 18 weeks.

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (CUSTOM HOUSE RULE)

Sleeper doesn't have native "steal a player from opponent" functionality. This is a **commissioner-enforced custom rule**.

**Recommended Flow:**
1. Every Monday after Week 1 scores finalize → Commissioner announces the new X Champion in league chat.
2. X Champion must publicly declare **which player** they are stealing from their upcoming opponent **before the Sunday 1pm ET lock**.
3. Commissioner manually tracks/adjusts the X Champion's flex scoring for that week (or the league agrees to honor the stolen player's points in the X holder's flex slot).
4. If X Champion loses → New champion is crowned. Repeat.

This can live as a fun/power layer on top of standard Sleeper scoring or be fully integrated into weekly standings via manual adjustment. Your call as commissioner.
